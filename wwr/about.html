Our History

How We Started

Our company began with a small group of friends and one worn-out raft. What started as weekend trips down the river grew into a full-time passion for sharing the wild water with others.

Growing Our Crew

Over the years we trained expert guides, upgraded our safety gear, and expanded our trips to fit every skill level, from relaxed half-day floats to full-day adventures through big rapids.
